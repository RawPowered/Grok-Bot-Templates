# Grok Bot: Content Team Group Chat

Members:
- Strategy/analytics bot: slot plan, reply targets, results reports.
- Content bot: finds clips, facts, Easter eggs with sources.
- Reply bot: drafts reply options.
- Producer bot: final drafts and scheduling.
- You: approve everything.

Flow:
1. Strategy sets plan. 2. Content and reply bots draft. 3. Producer verifies and assembles final drafts. 4. You approve exact wording. 5. Producer schedules and confirms.
- Bots stay quiet unless they add something. One "pause" stops all routines.

## About schedule times
Any times in this template are examples. Set your own times to fit your posting needs.
