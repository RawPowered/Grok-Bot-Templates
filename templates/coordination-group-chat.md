# Grok Bot: Coordination Group Chat

Members: a work and email assistant and a second assistant (for example, applications and outreach).

## Purpose
- Keeps the owner and both bots on the same page at the same time.
- The owner posts one update so it reaches both bots at once, and they can act on it together.
- The bots use the room to stay in sync with each other, so they don't duplicate work or work from outdated info.

## How it runs
- The owner's message in the room is the shared instruction for both bots.
- Each bot acts on its own part and confirms in the room.
- Anything that goes out under the owner's name still needs the owner's approval first.

## About schedule times
Any times in this template are examples. Set your own times to fit your work schedule and outreach preferences.
